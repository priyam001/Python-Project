Problem Statement: News Category Classification
=================================================

Can you categorize news articles based on their headlines and short descriptions?

Do news articles from different categories have different writing styles?

A classifier trained on this dataset could be used on a free text to identify
the type of news it represents, enabling automated content categorization.

This has practical applications in:
  - Automated news feed organization
  - Content recommendation systems
  - Media trend analysis
  - Audience targeting for publishers
  - TRP prediction based on news category preferences

The goal is to build a machine learning model that accurately predicts the
category of a news article given its headline and a brief description.
